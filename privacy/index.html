What this site collects, in a few sentences

This site uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, both through Google Tag Manager, to see which pages get read, for how long, and where people drop off. Neither collects names, emails, or any account information unless you send them to me directly.

I've set defaults meant to limit tracking for visitors in the EU, UK, and Switzerland, but I can't guarantee every tag on this site honors that in every case.
